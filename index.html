CompletedResearch project

Autonomous UAV Swarm Navigation Based Visual SLAM: Concept and Design

Robotics | Computer Vision | Autonomous Systems | Simulation

Designed and studied autonomous navigation for a two-UAV swarm using camera-based visual SLAM in simulation.

ROS 2 HumbleGazeboArduPilot SITLMAVLink / MAVProxyORB-SLAM3 / RTAB-MapRViz2Python

View Project

Objective. The project investigated how camera-based perception, localization, mapping, and robotic simulation can support autonomous navigation and coordination of two UAVs.

Why it is hard. UAVs have limited payload and compute, move fast in three dimensions, and often cannot rely on GPS indoors or in cluttered areas.

Role of visual SLAM. It estimates a vehicle’s pose and builds a map from camera images alone, which supports navigation without external positioning.

Purpose of simulation. Gazebo with ArduPilot SITL allows safe, repeatable testing before any hardware is considered.

Environment and workflow. The simulation used two drones with RGB cameras connected through ROS 2 and MAVLink. The workflow covered image capture, SLAM-based localization and mapping, and navigation commands visualized in RViz2.

Status. Thesis defended in June 2026.
